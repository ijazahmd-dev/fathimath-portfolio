Search moved from ten blue links to one good answer. Answer engine optimization (AEO) is how your brand becomes that answer: the entity work, structured data, factual clarity, digital PR, and weekly monitoring that get you cited across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

The best AEO agencies in 2026 do the work, not just the audit.. I ranked all agencies on my list on AI engine coverage, execution, IP, technical depth, and real-world proof.

Start with the comparison table, then read the notes: what each team fixes, how they measure, and where they fit. I judged all ten on the same five criteria.

How I ranked

I reviewed public artifacts (service pages, research, frameworks, talks, and case studies) and weighted the things that correlate with LLM visibility and revenue:

  1. AI Engine Coverage & Monitoring (25%) – proof the team tracks and improves visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, and retail AIs.
  2. Actionability & Execution (25%) – not just audits. Do they ship schema, content, entity fixes, and outreach?
  3. Innovation & IP (20%) – frameworks, scores, simulators, or platforms launched since 2024.
  4. Technical AEO (15%) – structured data depth, entity modeling, log analysis, content engineering.
  5. Real-World Proof (15%) – case studies, talks, independent coverage.

How to choose an AEO partner (quick buyer’s guide)

Prioritize these five signals:

  1. Live AI visibility monitoring, not just monthly slides. Ask to see your citation share, sources, and sentiment in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini today. (Platforms like Goodie and Luminr are examples, compare options in my AI search visibility monitoring tools guide.)
  2. Entity & schema expertise. Review a sample entity map, Organization/Product/FAQ/HowTo schema, and plan to reconcile conflicting brand facts across the web.
  3. Execution muscle. Who will actually ship structured data, content refactors, and outreach? You want engineers + editors + PR, not an “audit only” team.
  4. Research & IP since 2024. Fresh frameworks matter: Relevance Engineering, Everysearch™, or platform-native playbooks.
  5. Attribution plan. You won’t get perfect referral data from AI answers. You can triangulate with snapshots, branded search lift, assisted conversions, and pipeline. Ask to see their model.

RFP questions you can steal:

  • “Show us a 90-day AI visibility delta you achieved, with before/after snapshots with the sources cited by engines.”
  • “Share your entity consolidation checklist and how you resolve fact conflicts across third-party profiles.”
  • “Demo how your team tests prompt clusters and tunes on-page structure for extractability.”
  • “Map AEO work to pipeline: which answers and assets influenced SQLs or revenue last quarter?”

What to expect in an AEO scope (sane, practical version)

  • Week 1–3, baseline and fixes: LLM visibility audit; entity map; priority schema, fast content restructures (question-first headings, FAQs, summaries).
  • Month 2–3, authority and distribution: Digital PR and citations in sources models already trust (reviews, expert roundups, forums), plus answer-ready content refreshes.

FAQs (for your team & stakeholders)

Is AEO replacing SEO?
No. SEO keeps you discoverable; AEO ensures you’re the answer once discovered. They’re complementary.
What does an AEO agency do?
An AEO agency gets your brand cited in AI answers. In practice: it fixes your entities and structured data, engineers content into answer-shaped formats, earns citations through digital PR in sources models already trust, and monitors your citation share across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews week over week. The good ones ship the fixes. The weak ones sell you a dashboard and call it a strategy.
How are AEO services different from SEO services?
SEO earns rankings. AEO earns citations. SEO optimizes a page's position in the blue links; AEO optimizes whether a model names you in its answer. The mechanics overlap (clean structure, authority, fast pages), but AEO adds entity consolidation, schema built for retrieval, passage-level clarity, and prompt-based measurement instead of keyword-rank tracking. You run them together. See my best AEO tools breakdown for the software side.
Who needs an AEO agency?
Any brand whose buyers now start research inside an AI engine instead of Google. That's most B2B SaaS today, plus a fast-growing share of consumer and ecommerce. If prospects ask ChatGPT or Perplexity "what's the best [your category]" and you're not in the answer, you're losing pipeline you can't see in analytics. If your category is still low AI-intent, a good tool and a disciplined in-house process may be enough for now.
